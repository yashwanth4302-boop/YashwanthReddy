MY BIOGRAPHY

Yashwanth is a passionate graphic designer with over 3 years of experience in visual storytelling. His journey began with a deep fascination for colors, shapes, and creativity. He specializes in brand identity, digital graphics, and print media. With a strong base in digital media and design principles, he brings ideas to life with clarity and precision. Yashwanth’s design philosophy blends aesthetic beauty with strategic purpose. Every project he creates reflects emotion, balance, and meaning. He focuses on delivering designs that communicate, connect, and inspire. Driven by curiosity, he constantly explores new design trends and techniques. For him, design isn’t just about visuals — it’s about creating experiences. Through every detail, Yashwanth strives to make a lasting visual impact.

JOURNEY

YASHWANTH'S JOURNEY – A TIMELINE

YEAR — 2021
In 2021, Yashwanth discovered his passion for graphic design, marking the beginning of his creative journey. He started experimenting with visual compositions, color palettes, and digital art, exploring how design could tell stories without words. Each project became a step toward mastering the art of visual storytelling.
YEAR — 2022
Yashwanth pursued a degree in Fine Arts with a focus on graphic design at Pixxel Arts, refining his technical skills and artistic sensibilities. His education strengthened his understanding of design principles and deepened his passion for creating visuals with purpose.
YEAR — 2023
In 2023, Yashwanth started working in graphic design and video editing while learning UX/UI design at Pixxel Arts Studios, building his skills across visual and digital design.
YEAR — 2026
In 2026, Yashwanth completed his Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at R.G. Kedia College of Commerce, Hyderabad.
